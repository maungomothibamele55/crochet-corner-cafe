Tutorials

easy step by step guides to help you learn crochet from the very beginning

lets get started!

dont worry if you have never crocheted before. these tutorials are made for complete beginners. just follow the steps one by one and you will get the hang of it, it just takes a little bit of practice!

  • a crochet hook (size 4mm or 5mm is good for beginners)
  • yarn — any colour you like, medium weight is easiest
  • scissors
  • a yarn needle (for finishing off at the end)
  • patience and a bit of time to practice
  • thats it! you dont need much to get started
Tutorial 1

How to Crochet a Scarf

a scarf is one of the best first projects because its just one long rectangle. no complicated shapes or difficult stitches needed!

  1. start by making a slip knot on your hook
  2. chain stitch until your row is about 15 stitches long (this is the width of your scarf)
  3. do a single crochet stitch in each chain across the row
  4. chain 1 and then turn your work around
  5. single crochet across again, chain 1, and turn
  6. keep repeating this until your scarf is as long as you want it (about 150cm is good)
  7. when you are done, cut the yarn and pull the end through the last loop to fasten off
  8. use a yarn needle to hide the loose ends inside the scarf

Tip: dont pull your yarn too tight or it will be hard to put the hook through the stitches!

Tutorial 2

How to Crochet a Hat

a beanie hat is a bit harder than a scarf but its still very doable for beginners. you will work in circles for this one!

  1. make a magic ring — this is a loop that you can pull tight when done
  2. single crochet 6 stitches into the ring, then pull it closed
  3. in the next round, do 2 single crochets in every stitch (you should now have 12)
  4. in round 3, alternate between 1 normal stitch and 1 increase (you should get 18)
  5. keep adding rounds without increases until the hat is about 8cm tall
  6. continue until the hat fits your head (try it on as you go!)
  7. fasten off and tuck the ends in with your yarn needle

Tip: use a stitch marker to keep track of where each round starts. it helps a lot!

Beginner Tips

General Crochet Tips for Beginners

here are some helpful tips that will make your crochet journey easier and more enjoyable. read through these before you start!

  1. practice your tension — try to hold the yarn consistently so your stitches are even in size
  2. count your stitches — always count at the end of each row to make sure you havent missed any
  3. go slow — speed comes with practice, dont rush when you are learning
  4. use good lighting — it is much easier to see dark yarn in a bright room
  5. watch videos — sometimes its easier to understand a stitch by watching someone do it
  6. dont give up — everyone makes mistakes when learning. just undo it and try again!
  7. have fun — crochet should be relaxing and enjoyable, not stressful
